# Workstations

Every workstation is a post with a stock and a basket (see [Getting Started](Getting-Started)). Using a post opens its screen, where you choose what it keeps or grows. The odds of every drop show under the mouse. All times below are the defaults and can be changed in the [Configuration](Configuration).

## Crop Plot (Carved Stake)

Sows, waters and harvests. The soil stays wet with no water near.

- **Made with:** splinter, timber, crimson seal and a hoe. Set it at the edge of tilled soil.
- **Grows:** wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, pumpkins, melons. An apple in the stock is planted as a small apple tree that gives two. Bone meal in the stock is cast over the whole field at once.
- **The map:** use the stake to see the field. Click a square with a seed in hand and it is always sown with that. Click with an empty hand to keep a square bare (pumpkins and melons need one beside them). Right-click to fix what grows there now. The number shown is the share of seed kept back for sowing.
- **Trellis:** a trellis in the field holds glow berries up to three high. The worker sets the vine and picks the berries.

## Ranch (Hitching Post)

Animals give their drops without being hurt.

- **Made with:** a lead. Fence a pen from it and lead animals in.
- **The herd:** animals led into the pen join the herd. One pale green shape per kind wanders the pen. They cannot be hurt. Up to 32 per ranch. The post keeps its herd if you move it.
- **Feed:** a **Trough** holds their feed, and the worker refills it from the stock.
- **Drops:** each grown animal gives what it would have dropped every two minutes, for one piece of its feed. Cows and goats are milked if there are buckets in the stock. A mooshroom fills bowls.
- **Breeding:** use the post to breed a pair (four of their feed) or to set one free.

## Orchard (Woodsman's Post)

- **Made with:** an axe. Fence in dirt.
- **Work:** plants saplings from the stock on every other square like a chessboard, fells grown trees whole (leaves too) and plants again.
- **Also grows:** cactus on sand, bamboo and sweet berries. A cactus in flower is cut for the flower.

## Pond (Angler's Post)

- **Made with:** a fishing rod. Fence in water, at least 4 squares of it.
- **Work:** fishes from the top of the post and plants sugar cane on the banks. Use the post to see everything it can bring up and turn off what you do not want. What it throws back still costs the cast. Sneak-use the post for the map of the banks.
- **Bait** (in the stock, one per cast):

| Bait | Effect |
|---|---|
| Bait | a fifth quicker, no more boots |
| Fine Bait | quicker again, some treasure |
| Rift Bait | less than half the time, nearly one catch in five is treasure |

## Flower Garden (Gardener's Post)

- **Made with:** any small flower. Fence in grass.
- **Work:** flowers from the stock are planted and stay. Each gives a bloom every two minutes, twice as often with bone meal. Use the post to grind any kind into dye as it is cut.

## Apiary (Beekeeper's Post)

- **Made with:** a beehive. Fence in a yard, set the hives and plant flowers yourself.
- **Work:** hives fill with no bees in them, quicker with more flowers (up to 11). Use the post to choose what a full hive becomes: honey into a cask, honey bottles if there are bottles in the stock, or honeycomb. A bottle used on a cask of honey fills.

## Gilded Garden (Gilder's Post)

- **Made with:** a golden carrot. Fence in tilled soil.
- **Grows:** golden carrots, golden apples and glistering melon slices, each coming back as two (or as a melon of slices). Enchanted golden apples grow too, slowly.

## Mushroom Hollow (Forager's Post)

- **Made with:** a mushroom, under the violet seal.
- **Structure:** a cave you build, 5 x 5 and 5 high, of cobblestone, stone, deepslate, tuff or moss, with the stock, post and basket in front. When the last block is laid it grows over.
- **Work:** mushrooms come up on the bed by themselves, one every 20 seconds. Bone meal fills the bed at once.
- **Spore Loam:** takes every kind: mushrooms, both nether fungi, nether wart. Keep one of a kind in the stock and use the post to choose what grows.

## Mine (Miner's Post)

- **Made with:** a pickaxe. Pickaxes of any kind go in its stock.
- **Structure:** stone walls with logs in front.
- **Work:** nothing is dug from the world. Ore shows on the walls, the miner breaks it, and the wall is rock again. Use the post to set the height it digs at (down to the bottom of the world wherever the mine stands) and to turn off ores you do not want. Torches make it quicker. Fortune and Silk Touch on the pickaxe count.
- **Lava:** a [Pump](Casks-Pump-and-Cord) in the middle of the floor draws lava into casks at the very bottom of the world.

## Nether Mine (Nether Miner's Post)

- **Made with:** netherrack, and a shaft of nether stone in the same shape as the mine.
- **Work:** quartz, nether gold, glowstone, and now and then ancient debris. It works in any dimension. A pump works at any height here.
