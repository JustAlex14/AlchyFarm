# Stamps and the Rift

## Stamps

A stamp pressed on a post changes how the place is worked. Sneak-use the post with a stamp, or use the letter at the right of its screen. A post bears one stamp of each kind, and the one it replaces comes back to you.

| Stamp | Effect by tier (I, II, III, IV) |
|---|---|
| Haste | work, walking and anything that ripens by the clock, quicker by 15, 30, 55, then 80 percent |
| Bounty | more brought back: one more for every four, then for every two, then more than double |
| Thrift | less used up (seed, bone meal, bait, feed, pickaxe wear): spared 20, 40, 65, then 90 times in a hundred |
| Quality Work | the post is never short: bone meal, torches, bait and feed work as if the stock were full, and none is taken |
| Void | seeds and saplings beyond what is kept for sowing are destroyed, not stored. Tier II keeps only what the rift has touched |

### Tiers

- **Tier II** is the stamp again, with a second crimson seal.
- **Tier III** takes a violet seal.
- **Tier IV** takes another violet seal, a nether star, and something deep-rifted: an item brought back by a post that already bears three violet stamps.

## Working by the rift

Tiers III and IV are violet stamps, and they change the worker. It stops walking, raises its arms, and a tear opens in front of it: what it wanted comes to it, and the field ripens faster.

Each tier above II on each of Haste, Bounty and Thrift adds one to the post's **rift count**, from 1 to 6. At 6 the worker stands with its back to the post and never moves again: a field is reaped in one breath, the mine gives only what you asked for, the pond is fished everywhere at once.

## Rifted produce

Some of what a post brings back by the rift comes out **rifted**: six in a hundred for each point of the rift count. A rifted item carries a level and keeps it through a furnace.

- Food feeds twice, three or four times over, and does something to whoever eats it.
- Raw ore smelts into as much more.
- Books have more written in them.
- By the rift, the pond brings up potions nobody brewed, stronger than any you can make.
- Rifted honey, milk and mushroom stew each fill casks of their own, one per depth of the rift.
- The Mushroom Hollow and the Flower Garden give more, but nothing comes back changed.

Rifted items are an AlchyX feature, so other mods can use them in recipes (`alchyx:rifted` ingredient).

One note from the ledger: the plot sometimes returns a rifted poisonous potato. Do not eat it, and be careful what you brew with it.
