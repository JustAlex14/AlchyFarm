# Casks, Pump and Cord

## Cask

A cask holds 16 buckets of what a post gives as a liquid: lava, honey, milk and mushroom stew, and their rifted kinds. Stand it on a mine's stock or basket, or beside a fenced post. Pipes and buckets can take from it.

## Pump

A pump in the middle of a mine's floor draws lava into the casks. It only works at the very bottom of the world in a plain Mine, or at any height in a Nether Mine. It never runs dry.

## Surveyor's Cord

For when the post should stand at your door and the field somewhere else.

1. Sneak and use the cord on a post to take hold of it. It glows while it holds one.
2. Still sneaking, use it on a stock, basket, trough or cask to join it to that post.
3. Use it on two corners of ground to mark the post's field between them.

Everything must be within 11 blocks of the post and on its level. Motes show what is bound while the cord is in hand. Using the cord on the same post again makes the post forget all its links. Sneak-use it in the air to let go of the post.
