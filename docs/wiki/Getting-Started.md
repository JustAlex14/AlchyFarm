# Getting Started

## Materials

Every post is made from the same few pieces:

| Item | Made from | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Carved Splinter | any log cut round an amethyst shard | the heart of every post |
| Estate Timber | planks banded with an iron nugget (4 per craft) | every post and store |
| Iron Fitting | an iron ingot blacked over coal (4 per craft) | corners, bands, hinges |
| Ledger Page | paper ruled in ink (2 per craft) | seals are set on it |
| Crimson Wax | honeycomb and red dye | the crimson seal |
| Crimson Seal | crimson wax on a ledger page | the first posts and tier I and II stamps |

Later posts and stamps need the **Violet Seal** (violet wax with an ender pearl pressed in it). Violet wax is honeycomb, an amethyst shard and obsidian.

## A workstation

A workstation is a **post**, a **Stock** and a **Basket**.

- The **Stock** holds what the post uses: seeds, bone meal, tools, bottles, bait.
- The **Basket** takes what it yields, and shows what is in it.
- Both must touch the post, or stand in the line of its fence. Several of each are allowed.
- A full basket stops the work. Nothing is ever thrown on the ground: the worker holds what does not fit and waits. Empty the basket, or put a hopper under it.

## The worker

Soon after a post is set up, a lost soul comes through a tear in the air and starts working it. One worker per post. It walks to each task, kneels, casts, and carries the harvest back. You do not give it orders: you give it a place and what the place needs.

## Fields

Most posts work a field:

- Close a fence round the ground from the post: that is the field, any shape, up to 9 x 9. Fence gates work. Hedges, glass panes, iron bars, trapdoors and carpets close it too.
- With no fence at all, a post works the 5 x 5 around itself.
- An open fence works nothing until it is closed.

The Mine, the Nether Mine and the Mushroom Hollow are fixed structures instead. Their shape is in the ledger and its Visualize button.

If the post should stand somewhere else than its field (at a door, for example), use the [Surveyor's Cord](Casks-Pump-and-Cord).

## The first one

Start with the **Crop Plot**: craft a Carved Stake (splinter, timber, crimson seal and a hoe), set it at the edge of tilled soil, add a stock with seeds and a basket. See [Workstations](Workstations).
