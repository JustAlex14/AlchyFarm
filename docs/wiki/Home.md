# AlchyFarm

AlchyFarm is a farming mod for NeoForge (Minecraft 26.1.2), built on [AlchyX](https://github.com/JustAlex14/AlchyX). You set a post in the ground, give it a stock and a basket, and a lost soul comes to work it: sowing, herding, fishing, mining. Then you seal it with violet stamps, and the soul starts working by magic.

## Pages

- [Getting Started](Getting-Started): materials, posts, stocks, baskets and fields
- [Workstations](Workstations): all ten, what they need and what they give
- [Stamps and the Rift](Stamps-and-the-Rift): Haste, Bounty, Thrift, Quality Work, Void, and rifted produce
- [Casks, Pump and Cord](Casks-Pump-and-Cord)
- [Configuration](Configuration)
- [Modpack Guide](Modpack-Guide): recipes, tags, costs and shapes for pack makers

## Requirements

- NeoForge 26.1.2
- [AlchyX](https://github.com/JustAlex14/AlchyX) 0.2.1 or newer
- Optional: Patchouli (the Estate Ledger), JEI (recipes and workstation pages), Jade

## The Estate Ledger

With Patchouli installed, the Estate Ledger is the in-game guide, kept by a nobleman sent to a land where nothing grew. Its paper changes as the story turns.
